**Topic 1**


You cannot wake up one day and decide that I want to analyse data.
You need to have a set of questions that you want to answer and a data analysis operation will give you evidence backed evidence .
          
           
**CASE STUDY**

           
I went to analyse my MPESA statement for two semesters .
The only question I had was how much money do I spend for each semester ?
That is vague , =SUM(E:E) operation is enough to answer that .
But along the way I got curious:

How much do I spend for data ?
For food?
How much do I save ?
How much goes in just for transaction cost ?
What about retail therapy?
